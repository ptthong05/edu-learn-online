# Register & Login

## Creating an Account

1. Navigate to `/register` or click **Sign Up** in the Header
2. Fill in your details: Full name, Email, Password
3. Click **Register**
4. You are automatically logged in and redirected to the homepage

## Logging In

1. Navigate to `/login` or click **Login** in the Header
2. Enter your Email and Password
3. Click **Login**

## Forgot Password

1. Click **Forgot password?** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your inbox for the reset link
4. Click the link and set a new password

## Logging Out

- Click your **Avatar** in the top-right corner of the Header
- Select **Logout**
