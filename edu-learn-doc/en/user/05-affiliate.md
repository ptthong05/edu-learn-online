# Affiliate Program

## What is Affiliate?

The Affiliate (Referral) Program lets you earn commissions when you refer people to buy courses through your personal link.

## How to Join

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Account → Affiliate**
3. Click **Join Now** (or contact Admin)
4. Receive your personal **Referral Code**

## Using Your Affiliate Link

Your affiliate link looks like:
```
https://edulearn.vn/?ref=YOUR_CODE
```

Share it on:
- Social media (Facebook, TikTok, YouTube)
- Personal blog
- Learning groups and communities

## Commission Structure

| Event | Commission |
|-------|------------|
| User purchases a course via your link | X% of order total |
| Applies to both individual courses and Combos | ✅ |

> The commission rate is configured by the Admin. View the current rate on your Affiliate page.

## Requesting a Withdrawal

### Requirements
- Minimum balance: As set by Admin

### Steps
1. Go to **Account → Affiliate → Request Withdrawal**
2. Enter the withdrawal amount
3. Enter your bank account details
4. Click **Submit Request**
5. Wait for Admin approval (usually 1–3 business days)

## Performance Tracking

The Affiliate page shows:
- **Total Clicks**: Number of times your link was clicked
- **Orders**: Successful orders from your link
- **Total Revenue**: Revenue generated
- **Pending Commission**: Commission not yet withdrawn
- **Withdrawn**: Amount already received
